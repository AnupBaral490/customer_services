# Review Section Visibility - Complete Guide

## ✅ Where Reviews Are Now Visible

### 1. **"Share Your Experience" Section** (ALWAYS VISIBLE)
- **Location:** Home page, between "How It Works" and testimonials sections
- **Status:** Shows on home page 24/7
- **Content:**
  - Heading: "Share Your Experience"
  - Description: "Help other customers by sharing your honest feedback"
  - Two buttons:
    - 🌟 "Submit Your Review" (Green - primary action)
    - ❓ "How It Works" (Secondary - educational)
  - Feature highlights:
    - Takes only 2 minutes
    - No login required
    - Your review helps others

### 2. **Customer Reviews/Testimonials Section** (CONDITIONAL)
- **Location:** Below "Share Your Experience" section
- **Status:** Only shows when there are approved reviews
- **Content:** Up to 6 latest approved customer reviews with:
  - Star ratings
  - Customer names
  - Service types
  - Review text
  - "Submit Your Review" CTA button

### 3. **In Navigation/Footer**
- Links to review pages in navigation if configured
- "Submit Review" links in footer if configured
