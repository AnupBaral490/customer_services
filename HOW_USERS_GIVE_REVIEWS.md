# How Users Give Reviews and Ratings - Complete Guide

## Overview
Users can easily submit reviews and ratings through a dedicated review page. The process is simple, transparent, and encouraged throughout the website.

## 📍 Where to Find Review Options

### 1. Home Page
- Scroll down to **"What Our Clients Say"** section
- Click green button: **"Submit Your Review"**
- Also shows previous customer reviews and ratings

### 2. Review Submission Page
- **URL:** `/testimonials/submit/`
- Direct page for submitting new reviews
- Form guide: "Learn how to submit a review" link at bottom

### 3. How-To Guide Page
- **URL:** `/how-to-review/`
- Comprehensive guide with step-by-step instructions
- Shows all the details about the review process
- Link available on submit form and throughout site

## 🎯 Step-by-Step Guide for Users

### Step 1: Navigate to Review Form
**Option A - From Home Page:**
- Scroll to testimonials section
- Click green "Submit Your Review" button

**Option B - Direct Link:**
- Go to `/testimonials/submit/`

**Option C - Learn First:**
- Go to `/how-to-review/` to understand the process
- Then click "Submit Your Review Now" CTA

### Step 2: Fill "Your Information" Section
Fields to complete:
- **Full Name** (required)
  - Example: "Sarah Johnson"
  - Used to display on website

### Step 3: Select Service and Rating

**Service Selection:**
- Enter service name used
- Examples:
  - Residential Cleaning
  - Commercial Cleaning
  - End-of-Lease Cleaning
  - Office Cleaning
  - Deep Cleaning
  - Window/Glass Cleaning
  - Carpet Cleaning

**Rating Selection:**
- 5 Stars ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Excellent, highly recommend!
- 4 Stars ⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Great, very satisfied
- 3 Stars ⭐⭐⭐ - Good, satisfied
- 2 Stars ⭐⭐ - Okay, could be better
- 1 Star ⭐ - Poor, not satisfied

**How to Select:**
- Click on the star rating radio button
- Stars highlight when you hover
- Your selection shows with green stars

### Step 4: Write Your Review
**What to Include:**
- What you liked about the service
- Quality of cleaning
- Professionalism of the team
- Punctuality/timing
- Would you recommend us?
- Any specific feedback

**Example Review:**
> "Excellent service! The team was professional, punctual, and thorough. They cleaned every corner of our home and left it spotless. Very impressed with their attention to detail. Highly recommend!"

**Tips:**
- Be honest and specific
- Share your genuine experience
- Keep it constructive
- Professional tone works best
- Minimum a few sentences recommended

### Step 5: Submit Your Review
- Click green "Submit Review" button
- Form validates all required fields
- If errors, see red messages and fix them
- Once submitted → redirected to success page

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to log in to submit a review?
**No** - You can submit reviews without creating an account. Just provide your name.

### Can I edit my review after submitting?
Currently, you cannot self-edit. Contact us if you need to make changes and we'll help update it.

### Will you reject my review?
We welcome honest feedback (positive or negative). We only reject reviews that are:
- Abusive or offensive
- Spam or advertising
- Inappropriate content
- Unrelated to our services

Fair criticism helps us improve and is valued.

### Can I submit multiple reviews?
Yes! If you've used different services or had multiple experiences, please submit separate reviews for each. We love hearing about all your experiences.

### How long until my review appears?
Approved reviews typically appear within 24-48 hours of submission. You'll see it on the home page in the testimonials section.

### Where will my review appear?
Approved reviews appear in:
- Home page testimonials section ("What Our Clients Say")
- Randomly rotated among other reviews
- Some featured prominently based on rating

### Can I see my review after it's approved?
Yes! Scroll to the testimonials section on the home page and you'll see your review displayed with your name and service type.

### Is my email or personal data shared?
We only ask for your name. We never share personal information. Your name is used only to display your review.

## 🎓 What Makes a Great Review

### Good Review Characteristics
- ✅ Specific and detailed
- ✅ Mentions actual experience
- ✅ Professional tone
- ✅ Honest assessment
- ✅ Constructive feedback
- ✅ Would you recommend? Yes/No

### Example Good Reviews
> "Great service! Arrived on time, team was professional and thorough. Our home was spotless after. Very satisfied!"

> "Excellent deep cleaning. They paid attention to details we often miss. Professional, friendly, and efficient. Highly recommend!"

> "Good job overall. Room was cleaned well, but took longer than expected. Still satisfied with the result."

### Avoid
- ❌ Personal attacks
- ❌ Unrelated comments
- ❌ Advertising other services
- ❌ Contact information in review
- ❌ All caps
- ❌ Offensive language
