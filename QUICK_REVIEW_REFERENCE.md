# Quick Review Reference - At a Glance

## 🎯 For Customers: How to Submit a Review

```
STEP 1: Find the Review Button
   └─ Home page → Scroll to "What Our Clients Say"
   └─ Click green button "Submit Your Review"
   └─ Or go directly to /testimonials/submit/

STEP 2: Fill Your Name
   └─ Enter your full name (required)

STEP 3: Choose Service & Rating
   └─ Service: "Residential Cleaning" (example)
   └─ Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (select 1-5 stars)

STEP 4: Write Your Review
   └─ Share your honest experience
   └─ What did you like?
   └─ Would you recommend us?

STEP 5: Submit
   └─ Click "Submit Review" button
   └─ See success page confirmation

STEP 6: Wait for Approval
   └─ Admin reviews (24-48 hours)
   └─ Approved reviews appear on website
```

## ❓ Quick FAQ

**Q: Do I need to log in?**
A: No, anyone can submit a review

**Q: How long until my review appears?**
A: 24-48 hours after admin approval

**Q: Can I edit after submitting?**
A: Contact us and we'll help update it

**Q: Will negative reviews be rejected?**
A: No, honest feedback is welcome. Only spam is rejected

**Q: Can I submit multiple reviews?**
A: Yes, one for each service/experience

## 👥 For Admin: Quick Dashboard

**Access Reviews:**
- Go to `/admin/`
- Find "Core" → "Testimonials"

**Approve Reviews:**
- Click review → Check "Is Approved" → Save

**Delete/Reject:**
- Click review → Click "Delete" → Confirm

**View Pending:**
- Filter by "Is Approved" = "No"
