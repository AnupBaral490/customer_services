About Our Reviews

Are these real customer reviews?

Yes! All reviews are submitted directly by customers. We verify submissions to ensure authenticity and quality.

How are reviews approved?

We review all submissions within 24-48 hours. We approve honest feedback (positive or negative) and reject only spam or inappropriate content.

Can I trust the ratings?

Absolutely. Each review includes the customer name, service type, date, and detailed feedback. This transparency builds trust.

How often are reviews updated?

New approved reviews are added regularly. The most recent reviews appear first to show current customer experiences.
